Verb forms

(i) Imperative/present indic.

triallmaid (II, 1) - imperative, or 1 pl. pres. indic.?

(ii) Pres./fut. confusion

Pres. indic. used as future

marbhmai[d]-n[e] (II, 11, and similar forms in 13, 23)

cha dtig (II, 22, 24)

cha deyd - cha dtéid (IX, 227)

Future used as pres.

adéar-sa (II, 29)

Loss of future stems

MS Ne hy'nassit - ní h-<inneósad> (VI, 19)

but retained

MS Ni hy'nosit (VI, 35)


(iii) Erosion of subjunctive forms

MS Dane vaga - D n bhfaicthea (III, 9)

MS Da wacca - Da <bhfaicthea> (XI, 81, 129)


(iv) Retention of subjunctive forms

MS gi [superscr.] wayassi - go bhfaghasa (VI, 66)

MS Mir a 3ana ma - Mura dhéana mé (VI, 61)

MS a waym bas - [d]a bhfagham bás (VI, 154)

MS Co westmist - go bhfeasmaois (VII, 39)


(v) Unusual forms of endings

1 pl pres. indic.

MS Marmy' – Marbhmai[d]-n[e] (II, 11)

MS Tuttmy' - tuitmi[d]-n[e](II, 23)

MS varmon - mharbhmai[d]-n[e] (II, 13)


(vi) Loss of -g of ag before vb n

MS a doll - a' dol (V, 16, 18)

MS a moskleit - a' mosgladh (V, 40)

MS a brat a 3illi - a' breith (VI, 64)

MS a tarring clochk - a' tarraing cloch (IV, 27)

MS a shelgi - a' sealg (XVII, 79)


(vii) Copula

(a) dob becomes a b'

MS a barri - a b'fhearr (V, 29, 46)

MS a bar - a b'fhearr (XIII, 103)

but retained -

MS di balin - do b' álainn (V, 36)

MS di bimmi - do b'iomadh (V, 38)

MS di bi raa - do bu réidh (VI, 44)


(vii) Substantive

Classical form of 3 sg preterite retained –

MS di weit - do bhí (V, 50)

MS di wee - do bhí (X, 86)

Bha (as in Scottish Gaelic)

MS ne wai 3aw - na bha dh' ágh (VI, 12)

MS a waa marve - a bha marbh (VI, 122)


(viii) Relative do reduced to a

MS Noyr a hwyt - 'Nuair a shuidheadh (V, 37)

MS a roynit - a rinne (V, 57)

MS a ronyt - a rinne (V, 65)

MS a l<e>gga woyn (VI, 140)

MS Nor a 3oyf - 'Nuair a ghabh (VII, 13)

MS Mor <a> chonik - Mar a chunnaic (VII, 45)

MS mir a weissit - mar a bhíos-sa (XI, 105)

MS a ne - a ní (XI, 62)

MS a quhym< > - a chuadhm<ar> (XVI, 1)

MS a 3arwe - a dhearbhadh (XVI, 124)


(ix) Loss of relative do

MS dwn we ann - duin[e] [a] bhí ann (XVI, 3)


(x) Duplication of preverb do

MS di 3agamir - do dh'fhágamar (VI, 91, 99)

MS a 3aik sin - a dh'fhág sinn (VI, 96)

MS a chasgir sin - a chasgair sinn (VI, 102)

MS di 3aig sin - do dh'fhág sinn (VI, 107)

MS say 3aigsin - 's é dh'fhág sinn (VI, 138)

MS a dowri - a d'fheóraich (IX, 94)

MS di 3obbredir - do dh'fhóbradar (IX, 212)


(xi) Lenition of preverb do to dh’

MS 3arremay - dh'áirmheadh (VI, 141)

MS 3earemir - Dh'iarramar (XVI, 5)

MS 3earemar - Dh'iarramar (XVI, 121)

but

MS Dearemar - D' iarramar (XVI, 9, and indicated by abbreviations in XVI, 13, 17, 21, 25, 29)


(xii) Reduction of preverb do

MS a saych - a s[h]aich (VII, 72)

MS (a le<n>s a har) - <do leanas-sa siar> (IX, 41)


(xiii) Loss of preverb do

MS chur - [do] chuir (VIII, 18)


(xiv) Retention of preverb do in dependent position

MS nat dorrimir - nach d'fhuaramar (XVI, 11)

MS nat d[ ] - nach d'[fhuaramar] (XVI, 11, 15, 19)

but

MS nat forrimir - nach fuaramar (XVI, 7)


(xv) Lenition of past pass. following preverb

MS di churri - do chuireadh (VIII, 15)


(xiv) Analytic verb forms

MS Neir cleacht m‚ - Níor c[h]leacht mé (IX, 5)

MS Di (royn missin) - Do rinn mé sin (IX, 13)

MS Char 3ult ay - Char dhiúlt ‚ (XIII, 61)
