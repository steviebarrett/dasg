                      Ard aigne Ghuill


Translation:

The author of what follows is Fearghus the Poet

1. “High is Goll’s spirit; he fights against Fionn; he is a tall fierce warrior, whose foray is not feeble.
2. “He has the exalted dignity of a noble; his figure is nobly shaped; fair-formed comely Goll is the leader of a host.
3. “The lively Son of Morna was brave in battle; his fame was not recent; he is a congenial fellow.
4. “He is the generous king of a tribe; his speech is not weak; his mind does not mislead; he is a great cheerful hero.
5. “When he enters battle, he advances like a prince; though his skin is smooth, his deeds are hard.
6. “His stature is not small, [like] a tower above trees; he is of purest temperament, the teacher of the schools.
7. “He is <[like] an eagle> above the mountain-tops in the region; we are afraid lest he should lodge a claim against us.
8. “I tell you, Fionn of the brown locks, that your spirit is heavy for fear of Goll.
9. “It is certain that in battle he will add to his prowess; the attacker <of all>, he is the controller of a host.
10. “His status is not slight; he is a man of bloodshed; the rewarder of the schools, he has a noble disposition.
11. “His <reputation> is glorious; he is liberal and generous; his battle-din is persistent; his mind has the stability of a prince.
12. “His hair is brown, his breast is like chalk, he is perfect in body, full of affection.
13. “To have Ireland under his tribute <would augment> his cause; he is habitually courageous; his countenance is comely.
14. “No king surpasses Goll – he does not hide it from you, Fionn; his fierce valour is as strong as the waves.
15. “He is princely also; his skin is well-coloured; do not come against Goll suddenly – in battle-ardour he is no weak man.
16. “He dispenses liberally; his energy is ferocious; he is <manly> and gentle, heroic and great.
17. “His prowess and his anger are perpetually <directed against his lord>; he is hostile to all – a ready-handed warrior.
18. “An enduring pillar of battle, he is the joy of the Fiana; he is <noisy and handsome>, <many-sided> and swift.
19. “He is [like] a lion in valour, and dauntless in deed; his arm is supple; he is the choicest of kings.
20. “There is joy in his path; his teeth are shining white; he will bring grief on every warrior, whatever his size.
21. “The man’s purpose is firm, and he wins triumph in combat; his valour is daring – a violent story.
22. “A <wound-dealing> fierce hero, he is deadly with a blade; Goll is haughty, we think, and selfish too.
23. “A warrior well-armed and bold, he is angry with <his faults>; his rage is ferocious; he is [like] a wolf-dog in valour.
24. “He is <the talk> of heroes, and greatly loved by women; there is a flawless poet-band always beside him.
25. “Against a bold foe, his fight is not faint; when he boasts about his spoils, his visage is bright.
26. “His form is strong, his fortune is auspicious; no river is mightier than his advance in battle.
27. “Morna’s impetuous son, he redeems his pledge; his speech is temperate; his might is enduring.
28. “A fierce furious lord, his manner is manly and generous; no base thing is near him; his bounty is renowned.
29. “He is the fine son of <Téide> who would not forsake a poet-band, and whose hand did not hold back weakly from the strife of kings.
30. “His rage is terrible, his attack is fierce; when his wrath kindles, give him a third part of the hunt.
31. “You strong son of Cumhall, promise and observe a lasting peace towards Goll without hatred or treachery.”
32. “Now for all my days he will have his <customary dues>; I shall give him without deceit a third of the hunt.”
33. “There is something more, better than the rest: for the sake of your honour, Fionn, give a third of the hounds to Goll.
34. “Give up your rage, Goll, and be peaceful towards us; for the rest of your life, you will have, without deception, a third of Fionn’s hunting.”
35. “I shall accept that, generous Fergus, and my surliness has gone; my mind is no longer ill-disposed.”
36. “He is a friend without guile; he has slender red lips; his dignity, his vigour and his fame are no secret.”
