Prepositions and pronouns

Prepositions


(i) Duplication

MS di 3eyich - do dh'fhiadhach (V, 12)

MS di 3anit - do dh'Fhiana (XVII, 32)

MS di 3orrin - do dh'fhoirinn (XVII, 32, 44)

MS di 3inn - do fh'Fhionn (XVII, 61, 69)

MS a 3illin mcmorn - a dh'Iollann mac Morn[a]


(ii) Lenition of initial

MS ne wai 3ay - na bha dh'ágh (VI, 12)

MS 3i cormik - dha C[h]ormac (IX, 50)

MS gar worrin - dhar bhfoirinn (X, 83)

MS 3a wil - dha bhfeil (XI, 96)


(iii) Reduction

do > a in infinitive constr.

MS a churri - a chur (VI, 11)



(iv) Duplication (in infinitive constr.)

MS di 3oill - do dh'ól (VII, 1)

MS 3oskla - [a] dh'fhosgladh (IX, 1)

 


Pronouns

MS say - sé (nominative) (VI, 1)

MS rug ay - rug é (VI, 3)

MS di hog ea - do thog é (VI, 88)

MS cha di leggi ay - cha do leig é (VI, 114)

MS Char 3ult ay - Char dhiúlt é (XIII, 61)

 

an becomes na

MS ne wai 3aw - na bha dh' ágh (VI, 12)

MS er a wll - air a bhfuil (IX, 72)

MS a vacca - a bhfaca (X, 92)
